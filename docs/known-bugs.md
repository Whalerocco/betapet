16. ~~Android: the page cannot be scrolled in portrait — noticed when creating a new game, where the bottom of the setup form is unreachable until you pinch-zoom slightly, and in a game, where an open history drawer runs past the bottom of the screen. Seen in both DuckDuckGo and Chrome. (Reported 2026-09-15 in play.)~~ Fixed 2026-09-15, and it was two things.

    The root cause of the unscrollable page was in `globals.css`: `html` carried `height: 100%` *and* `overflow-x: hidden`. The second forces the other axis from `visible` to `auto`, so `html` became a scroll container fixed at exactly one viewport tall with the document overflowing inside it. A desktop wheel scrolls that happily, which is why it never showed up in testing; Android does not, because the browser's own viewport scroller then has nothing to scroll and a touch drag on the page does nothing — and pinch-zooming made it work again because panning a zoomed page is the visual viewport's job, not the scroller's. `html` now has neither property; `body` keeps `overflow-x: hidden`, which propagates to the viewport the way the box model intends, and `min-height: 100dvh` replaces the percentage that needed a height to resolve against.

    The history drawer is the second half, and it is a deliberate trade rather than a bug in the pinning. The playing view is pinned so a mobile browser's address bar cannot shift the board out from under a finger mid-drag (item 10), but a player reading the history is not dragging anything — the same reasoning that already left the game-over screen un-pinned. So the view is pinned while the drawer is closed and not while it is open (`useHistoryDrawer`, shared by both playing screens), and the drawer starts **closed** on a phone: leaving it open by default would mean the view was un-pinned for most players most of the time, giving back the protection entirely. `e2e/viewport-fit.spec.ts` holds both halves in both engines.

    **Verified in Chromium and WebKit at a phone viewport, not on a physical Android device** — the reporter should confirm the original symptom is gone.
