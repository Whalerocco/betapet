7. Replace mode: a tile displaced this turn can end up replacing another tile anyway, by swapping rather than by placing directly. Place a tile on committed X (X goes to your hand), place another on committed Y, then drop X onto that second square: the swap (DEC-017) hands X the square's displacement, so X is now the tile replacing Y — which `placeTile` refuses outright if you try to put X straight onto an occupied square (`REPLACE_CHAINING_NOT_ALLOWED`). Confirmed reproducible in the engine on 2026-08-23; not seen in play, and it needs a specific three-step sequence.

   **Needs a decision, not just a fix.** `game-modifiers.md` section 7 says a displaced tile "cannot itself be used to displace another tile in the same turn". Whether this sequence breaks that is genuinely arguable: X does not take Y off the board — the second placement already did — X only moves onto a square that was standing in for Y. So the letter of the rule may be intact while the end state is one the direct route forbids. Worth settling as: does the restriction protect the *act* of displacing, or the *outcome* of a just-displaced tile standing in for another displaced tile? If it is the outcome, the chaining check in `placeTile`/`movePendingTile` has to look at the inherited `replacedTileId` on a swap, not only at a displacement being created.
